# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

WORLD Strategy is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community for all participants. We are dedicated to fostering a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Disability
- Ethnicity
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual identity and orientation

## Expected Behavior

We ask all community members to:

- **Be respectful**: Treat others with kindness and professionalism
- **Be inclusive**: Welcome diverse perspectives and backgrounds
- **Be constructive**: Provide helpful feedback and focus on ideas, not people
- **Be honest**: Share knowledge openly and admit mistakes
- **Be patient**: Remember we're all learning

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, abuse, or discrimination of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Spam or off-topic disruption
- Advocating for or protecting those who engage in the above
- Unwelcome sexual comments or advances
- Intentional intimidation or threats

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Report it**: Email [conduct@worldstrategy.dev](mailto:conduct@worldstrategy.dev) with details
2. **Confidentiality**: Reports are confidential and reviewed promptly
3. **Action**: Maintainers will investigate and take appropriate action

Violations may result in:
- Warning or temporary muting
- Permanent removal from the community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub issues, PRs, and discussions
- Discord/chat channels
- Community forums
- In-person events

## Questions?

Contact the maintainers or open an issue with `[CONDUCT]` tag.
